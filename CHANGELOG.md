# Changelog

## 0.1.7 (26 Nov 2026)

- Clearer licence: free on your own ideas and apps and on apps you build for clients, including paid work; selling it, or briefs made with it, as a product or service is not allowed.

## 0.1.6 (26 Nov 2026)

- The Cursor build line no longer names a mode, and says Cursor should keep asking before it runs anything.

## 0.1.5 (21 Nov 2026)

After building the round 3 prompts in Lovable (it built all three steps after two questions):
- Round 1 asks the business name and where it is, so the app has its name and time zone; step 1 starts with both.
- Rounds 2a and 2b ask whether anyone can sign up or only invited people, whether people can change their own details, and whether a leaver's details are deleted.
- Part rounds ask what details each main thing holds; a time limit gets one "is a few minutes late OK?" follow-up.
- "Before you start" covers a web address for sending emails, real email addresses for test accounts, and answering the builder's questions.

## 0.1.4 (21 Nov 2026)

After a final run and reader check (prompts now "Easy to use"):
- Step 1 says how the person running the app gets their powers; test accounts are made after step 1; a note on email and text services.
- Prompts use titles, not IDs; "Not in this version" says it was the founder's choice, includes options turned down, and is left out when empty.
- Budget and deadline only in the "For you" note; whole-app items that need a part go in that part's step.

## 0.1.3 (21 Nov 2026)

After a second round of runs and a reader test (summary "Easy to use", brief "Usable with effort", prompts close to "Hard to use"):
- Build prompts never build anything from an unchosen part, put each check in the step that builds what it needs, start with a "Before you start" note (test accounts, old phones, real personal details), and keep budget and deadline in a "For you" note.
- Questions a first-version item can't be built without are asked at the read-back; a second "I don't know" uses a clearly marked starting guess.
- The brief says the first version is the founder's choice and lists important items left out.
- Belief Check verdicts shown in plain words; "none" fields left out; brief part 9 is a short list, with how to test in the separate starter.
- Open choices at the read-back get their own messages; a gaps check finds steps nobody does.

## 0.1.2 (20 Nov 2026)

After an independent audit of three blind runs (no core rule broken; the stop held every time):
- Whole-app needs (sign-in, who sees what, what it keeps, devices, privacy, accessibility, look and feel) always apply and aren't prioritised; they go into step 1, with the budget as a limit.
- Build prompts restate only what the founder confirmed; no extra details, no decisions left to the builder. Open choices are settled at the read-back.
- "Whatever's normal" always gets the trade-off and a question.
- Round 2 is split into 2a and 2b; follow-ups count towards a round's 10 questions.
- "I want X" is recorded and quoted in priority reasons; the read-back lists whole parts left out.
- Recommendations only for real common defaults; questions end with "?"; letters only for "it varies" cases.

## 0.1.1 (20 Nov 2026)

After four blind runs and a resume test (picking up from a save block in a fresh chat worked):
- Open questions allowed for facts; general examples before the founder has described their idea.
- Whole-app requirements get IDs (R0.1...); size, budget, deadline and "not in" stay facts.
- Boundary line with the priority table and in the summary; legal note once per topic.
- Read-back points out dependencies and unclear picks; closing line after the last section.
